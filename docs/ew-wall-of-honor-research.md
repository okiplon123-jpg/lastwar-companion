## PART 1: Exclusive Weapon (EW) / Broń Ekskluzywna

### 1.1 What Is an Exclusive Weapon?

✅ Every UR (Ultra Rare) hero has a unique Exclusive Weapon — a separate equipment piece that amplifies the hero's core kit. It is NOT a standard gear slot item.

✅ EW introduces:
- Hero-specific improved skill effects (unique per hero)
- Increases to the hero's maximum skill level cap (30 → 40)
- A universal troop-type-wide stat bonus at Level 20 (7.5% ATK + DEF + HP for all heroes of that troop type)

---

### 1.2 Unlock Requirements

| Condition | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Hero rarity | **UR only** — EW does not exist for SSR heroes unless promoted to UR |
| Hero star level | **5 stars required** — no exceptions; having 500 shards without 5★ = still locked |
| Initial shard cost | **50 named hero-specific EW shards** (universal purple shards cannot unlock, only upgrade) |
| SSR→UR promotions | Mason (S1), Violet (S2), Scarlett (S3), Venom (S5) gain EW access after UR promotion |

✅ EW unlocks at Level 1 the moment you spend 50 shards on a 5★ UR hero.

---

### 1.3 Shard Costs Per Level (Confirmed)

✅ Source: lastwar.us/forums (community-verified), lastwargame.online upgrade table

| Level Range | Cost Per Level (shards) | Cumulative Total (from L1) |
|-------------|------------------------|---------------------------|
| L1 (unlock) | 50 (named shards) | 50 |
| L2–L4 | 20 each | 50 + 60 = 110 |
| L5–L9 | 40 each | 110 + 200 = 310 |
| L10–L14 | 60 each | 310 + 300 = 610 (milestone reached at 310) |
| L15–L19 | 100 each | 610 + 500 = 1,110 |
| L20 | 100 (last of L15–L19 band) | **~1,130 total to reach L20** |
| L21–L24 | 150 each | 1,130 + 600 = 1,730 |
| L25–L30 | 200 each | 1,730 + 1,200 = **2,880 total to L30** |

> **Key totals:**
> - **To unlock (L1):** 50 shards
> - **To reach L10:** ~310 shards cumulative
> - **To reach L20:** ~1,130 shards cumulative ✅
> - **To reach L30:** ~2,880 shards cumulative ✅
> - **L20→L30 alone:** ~1,750 additional shards ✅

### 1.4 Milestone Levels & What They Unlock

✅ Levels 1, 10, 20, and 30 are the four special milestone levels with dedicated skill unlocks.

| Milestone | What Unlocks | Stat Bonus Scope |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **L1** | First EW skill effect; baseline stat boost; hero visual changes | Hero only (hero-specific skill) |
| **L10** | Second skill milestone; significant scaling improvement to the hero's Tactics skill | Hero only (enhanced Tactics) |
| **L20** | Third skill milestone; **+7.5% ATK, DEF, HP to ALL heroes of the same troop type** | **Entire troop type** ✅ |
| **L30** | Final skill milestone; maximum power; strongest hero-specific mechanic unlocked | Hero + troop type |

> ⚠️ The exact nature of the L10 bonus skill varies by hero (hero-specific Tactics upgrade). The L20 7.5% troop-wide bonus appears to be a **universal formula shared by all EW** — the same 7.5% applies to every UR hero's EW regardless of hero identity.

---

### 1.5 The 7.5% Troop Type Bonus — Scope & Mechanics

✅ **Confirmed across all major guides:**

- The 7.5% bonus at EW L20 applies to **all heroes of the same troop type** in your battle formation
- Example: Kimberly (Tank) EW at L20 → **every Tank hero** in the squad gets +7.5% ATK, DEF, and HP
- The buff does NOT require those other heroes to have their own EW unlocked
- Scope = **entire troop type in battle**, not just the one hero

| Bonus at L20 | ATK | DEF | HP | Scope |
|-------------|-----|-----|-----|-------|
| Value | +7.5% | +7.5% | +7.5% | All heroes of same troop type (Tank / Aircraft / Missile) |

> ✅ This is widely confirmed as a universal shared formula — all UR EWs give the same 7.5% troop-wide bonus at L20, regardless of hero identity.

> ⚠️ These are approximate community-reported figures. Exact BP values depend on hero and troop stats. The 7.5% troop-wide bonus at L20 also contributes to BP of all heroes of that type, so the real BP gain from L20 is higher than just the milestone increment.

### 1.8 Post-Level-30: Over-Cap Enhancement Tracks

✅ After reaching L30, three sequential enhancement tracks unlock:

| Track | Unlock Condition | Cost Per Level | Max Levels | Total Shards |
|-------|-----------------|---------------|-----------|-------------|
| **Attack** | EW at L30 | 10 shards/level | 50 levels | ~500 shards |
| **HP** | Attack track reaches L10 | 10 shards/level | 50 levels | ~500 shards |
| **Defense** | HP track reaches L10 | 10 shards/level | 50 levels | ~500 shards |

> ⚠️ ~estimated: The 10 shards/level and 50-level cap per track come from community reports. Not confirmed from official/wiki sources. Total per complete track: ~500 shards. All three tracks: ~1,500 additional shards.

Every 50 levels within a track gives a "large stat bonus" per the forum data. The exact percentage per 50 over-cap levels is **not confirmed** in public guides.

### 1.9 Hero-Specific vs Universal Formula

| Aspect | Universal (same for all UR EWs) | Hero-Specific |
|--------|--------------------------------|---------------|
| Shard upgrade cost table | ✅ Yes — same costs L1–L30 | — |
| Skill cap increase (+1 per 3 levels) | ✅ Yes | — |
| L20 troop-type bonus (7.5% ATK/DEF/HP) | ✅ Yes — same for every EW | — |
| Skill effects at L1, L10, L20, L30 | — | ✅ Yes — unique per hero |
| Hero visual appearance changes | — | ✅ Yes |

> Summary: The **stat/cost formula is universal** across all UR EWs. The **skill effects are hero-specific** and differ per hero (e.g. Morrison's EW adds HP%-based damage ignoring defense; McGregor's adds Fury debuff stacks).

## PART 2: Wall of Honor / Ściana Chwały

### 2.1 What Is Wall of Honor?

✅ Wall of Honor is a **passive bonus system** providing permanent, team-wide stat bonuses. Heroes are added to the Wall and their entries can be leveled indefinitely using hero shards.

Key characteristics:
- Bonuses are **permanent** and **always active** regardless of which heroes are fielded in battle
- **No level cap** — progression is infinite ✅
- Bonuses scale upward every 50 levels

---

### 2.2 Unlock Requirements

| Condition | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Hero rarity | Hero must be **5 stars** |
| Building requirement | Corresponding troop-type building must be **Level 20** (Tank Center, Aircraft Center, or Missile Center) |
| Automatic | Hero is automatically added to the Wall upon meeting both conditions |

✅ Heroes are added automatically — no separate unlock action needed.

---

### 2.3 How to Gain Levels

✅ Wall of Honor levels are gained by investing **hero shards** into that hero's Wall of Honor entry:
- Hero-specific shards work
- Universal purple shards can substitute for any hero
- Each shard invested contributes to that hero's Wall level

> ❓ **Exact shards-per-level formula is NOT confirmed** in any public guide. No source provides a table showing "X shards = Y Wall levels." The mechanic described is: excess shards after star/promotions go to Wall of Honor, with no stated cost-per-level breakdown.

---

### 2.4 Bonus Progression — Every 50 Levels

✅ Confirmed universal rule: **Bonuses increase every 50 levels.**

Two bonus tiers exist based on hero rarity/type:

| Tier | Value per 50 Levels | Hero Examples |
|------|---------------------|---------------|
| Standard | **+0.50%** | Kimberly, Morrison, Schuyler, Fiona, Swift, Mason (UR), Marshall, Stetmann, DVA, Lucius, Tesla, Violet (UR), Murphy, Williams, Carlie, Adam, McGregor |
| Half-value | **+0.25%** | Monica, Richard, Scarlett, Farhad, Cage, Sarah, Maxwell, Braz, Esla, Venom |
| Troop Load | **+1.00%** | Loki, Gump, Ambolt, Kane |

> Note on Mason and Violet: As SSR they give +0.25%; after UR promotion they give +0.50%. Shards invested at SSR rarity are **fully refunded** at UR promotion.

### 2.6 Stat Scope: Global or Per Troop Type?

✅ Wall of Honor bonuses apply **globally to the relevant stat type, across all troop types** in all combat — not restricted to a specific troop formation.

- An ATK bonus from Mason's Wall of Honor applies to all Tank heroes in all battles
- Bonuses "stack with everything" — mono squad bonuses, skills, gear, research
- The bonus is active in every combat scenario (PvP, base defense, rallies)

> Note: "Global" here means always-on, not limited to when that hero is in the squad. Bonuses from different heroes of the same troop type all stack and apply simultaneously.

---

### 2.7 Maximum Level

✅ **No maximum level.** The Wall of Honor has unlimited progression.

Practical milestones:
- Every 50 levels = bonus increase of +0.25%, +0.50%, or +1.00% depending on hero
- A hero at Wall Level 100 has 2× the bonus of Wall Level 50
- A hero at Wall Level 250 has 5× the bonus of Wall Level 50

---

### 2.8 Interaction with Battle Power

✅ Wall of Honor bonuses "stack with everything" including all other multipliers.

> ❓ **Whether Wall of Honor level directly increases the Battle Power (BP) number displayed is not confirmed** in any guide. Sources focus on the practical combat value without specifying BP formula inclusion.

---

### 2.9 Strategic Notes (for calculator context)

- **Mason** is the recommended first priority: ATK bonus (most valuable), refund at UR promotion makes it "free," applies to all Tank heroes
- **Morrison/Schuyler** (Aircraft ATK) stack with each other — both +0.50% ATK per 50 levels = +1.00% combined per milestone
- **Troop Load heroes** (Loki, Gump, Ambolt, Kane) give the highest per-milestone bonus (+1.00%) but affect march capacity rather than combat power
- **Half-value heroes** (+0.25%) are lower priority
- Purple shards: concentrate on one hero rather than spreading across many (slower to hit 50-level milestones if split)
- UR promotion refunds apply to Mason (S1), Violet (S2), and Scarlett (S3+) — invest freely before promotion
